## 0. Purpose
Consolidated, single-source functional specification for all Detached Officer Arrangements (DOA001–DOA016) requirements. It integrates every prior DOA item (including DOA011 marked obsolete) into one implementation reference for SAP ECC (HR/Payroll) and OLIMS, ensuring no requirement is omitted or contradicted across master data, payroll, OLIMS, external earnings, and indexation.

## 1. Business Background
The Enhanced Police Support Scheme (EPSS) provides ongoing support to medically exited officers. Some are rehired as detached (non-employee) to continue EPSS case management and benefit calculations. Key realities driving this design:

- WC payments after termination come directly from insurer EML, keeping NSWPF payroll clear of post-termination WC amounts; only approved retros for pre-termination dates are processed in SAP against the original employee number so detached periods never trigger payroll.
- WC certificates keep arriving after termination and must be captured for statutory S36/S37 week tracking and ongoing case management; OLIMS has to accept uploads post-termination, validate that a detached record exists, link the original and detached personnel numbers, and let claims be set to “Reopen” with Period 1/2/3 substatuses so administration continues without creating payroll payments.
- Detached officers need clear segregation from active staff using a dedicated personnel number range (e.g., 5000xxxx), a non-employee position, and an ESG such as “EPSS On Duty Detached” (ESG XV fallback if needed); these identifiers consistently drive payroll rules, reporting filters, and downstream exclusions so detached officers are prevented from SAP access, NSWPF email, and directory listings while still being visible for EPSS administration.
- Payroll must suppress WT4901 (and any WC wagetypes) for all detached periods using ESG/number-range controls, while still permitting P905-driven retros on the original employee number for pre-termination dates; this keeps detached records free of WC payments yet allows legitimate retrospective adjustments to flow on the active record only, with errors logged if suppression fails.
- External WC earnings supplied by EML must be uploaded through OLIMS, validated for detached ESG/number range, and written into SAP as a new IT0015 “WC External Earnings” wagetype that offsets EPSS payments; controlled overrides for insurer adjustments are allowed via OLIMS or PA30, with one-per-day checks, payroll-lock blocking, and full audit of source, user, timestamp, and reason.
- Salary and benefit bases for detached officers are frozen from the last active IT0008 on the original record, retaining capacity utilisation; no Award pay rises apply after termination, but eligible pre-termination retros still flow through, and CPI-based (or approved index) annual increases must be applied to Period 2/3 entitlements and the maximum benefit. Indexation uses the scheme’s effective date and prior-year rate if the new rate is unavailable, feeds the seven-year clock for Period 2, and is audited for rate source, effective date, user, and any override.
- Week counting for statutory S36/S37 must not depend on WT4901 because it is suppressed post-termination; OLIMS uses WC certificates as the primary source and supplements with the IT0015 “WC External Earnings” entries uploaded from EML. Pre-termination periods can still reference WT4901 on the original number, but detached periods rely on certificates/external earnings on the detached record. All inclusions, late or out-of-sequence corrections, and exclusions are audit-trailed and reportable so statutory tracking remains accurate without payroll wagetypes on detached officers.
- Robust audit, reconciliation, and error handling are required across OLIMS, SAP, and EML: bulk WC certificate uploads must validate and log linkage to the correct detached number with clear errors for missing records, bad dates, or duplicates; late/out-of-sequence data requires documented manual steps to redirect IT0014 9WCO and certificate linkage with full audit; WT4901 suppression and any IT0015 “WC External Earnings” overrides must be restricted, logged in both systems, and included in reconciliations so unauthorised payments are prevented and all actions remain reportable for compliance.
- Cohorts: automation covers EPSS detached officers only—terminated officers rehired as non-employees in the EPSS scheme, typically using the dedicated 5000xxxx range (or manual assignment in that range with ESG XV if automation fails). PBRI detached officers (e.g., 3000 series) remain manual and outside automation, and active officers continue under standard SAP/payroll processes with no change.
- Systems: SAP ECC manages detached master data, number range/ESG, and payroll logic for IT0014/IT0015 so WC payments never post on detached records, while OLIMS handles certificate intake, status/substatus (“Reopen” Period 1/2/3), linkage of original/detached numbers, external earnings uploads, week counting, and audit trail. Together they prevent ineligible WC payroll, catch late/out-of-sequence data before misallocation, block detached officers from downstream access, and keep statutory week-count/reporting intact without WT4901 by using certificate and external-earnings indicators.

## 2. Scope
- In scope (DOA001–DOA016): The solution covers detached officer master data in SAP (dedicated number range such as 5000xxxx, ESG “EPSS On Duty Detached” or fallback, prior employee number stored in IT0105 subtype FREM, and downstream exclusion from SAP access/email). It includes OLIMS certificate intake (single/bulk) with detached-record validation, dual linkage, “Reopen” with Period 1/2/3, week-counting enablement, error messaging, and full audit. SAP changes include IT0014 9WCO on detached numbers that must never trigger WT4901, WT4901 suppression for detached periods, P905 retros only on the original number for pre-termination dates, and week counting via alternative indicators. WC external earnings from EML flow through OLIMS into a new IT0015 “WC External Earnings” wagetype with EPSS offset logic and controlled overrides. Salary/benefit bases derive from the last active IT0008 (capacity utilisation retained), with CPI/approved index applied annually to Period 2/3, no post-termination Award rises, and eligible pre-termination retros processed on the original employee number. Controls, audit, reporting, error handling, and remediation for late/out-of-sequence data span OLIMS, SAP, and EML reconciliations.
- Out of scope: SAP GUI/ESS/email or other downstream access for detached officers; any Award pay increases after termination; and any claims officer override of IT0008 (Basic Pay) for detached officers (DOA011 remains obsolete).

## 3. Key Definitions & Cohort Identification
- Detached officer (EPSS): former police officer terminated on medical grounds and rehired as a non-employee solely for EPSS support; post-termination WC payments come from EML, with only pre-termination retros processed in SAP payroll. Only this EPSS detached cohort is in scope for automation and new numbering.
- ESG/position: use a non-employee position and ESG “EPSS On Duty Detached” to distinguish EPSS detached officers; if a new ESG is not feasible, use ESG XV as fallback. ESG XV is currently used for both PBRI (3000 series) and EPSS detached (e.g., 3100/5000 ranges), but only the EPSS cohort is automated and subject to new controls; PBRI remains manual.
- Personnel number range: assign a dedicated range (e.g., 5000xxxx, max 8 characters) via PA40 automation where possible; if system setup uses a 3100 range for EPSS detached, ensure it uniquely flags the cohort. Manual assignment in the same range with ESG XV is allowed if automation is unavailable. PBRI detached (e.g., 3000 series) stays manual and out of automation scope.
- Prior employee link: store the prior employee number in IT0105 subtype FREM on the detached record so OLIMS and SAP can link history and case management (TBC in final design).
- Identifiers for logic/reporting/validation: ESG, position, and number range together drive WT4901 suppression, reporting filters, downstream exclusion, OLIMS validation (including certificate upload checks and bulk upload rules), and reconciliation/compliance reporting; identifiers must remain consistent and fully audit-trailed across SAP and OLIMS, with documented manual corrections for late/out-of-sequence scenarios.

## 4. Solution Overview
- Master data & hire: Detached officers are rehired as non-employees via PA40 (baseline PBRI ZM infotypes) into a dedicated non-employee position with ESG “EPSS On Duty Detached”; a dedicated personnel number range (e.g., 5000xxxx, or 3100 if configured) is auto-assigned with manual fallback in the same range using ESG XV, respecting the SAP 8-character limit. ESG/number range drive exclusion from SAP access, NSWPF email, and directories, and PBRI (3000 series) remains manual/unaffected. The prior employee number is stored on the detached record in IT0105 subtype FREM (TBC) to keep history and case linkage intact.
- OLIMS flows: OLIMS accepts single and bulk WC certificates post-termination, validates a detached record exists, and links each certificate to both the original and detached numbers. Claims can be set to “Reopen” with Period 1/2/3 substatus; week-count tracking continues based on certificate data and external indicators, with per-record validation, duplicate prevention, and clear error reporting for invalid dates/linkage.
- IT0014 / WT4901: IT0014 9WCO is created on the original number for pre-termination coverage and on the detached number only post-hire; WT4901 is suppressed for detached officers so no WC payroll is generated. P905-driven retros run only on the original employee number for pre-termination dates, and future-dated records are prevented from paying when inactive; late/out-of-sequence cases have documented manual correction steps.
- WC external earnings / IT0015: WC payments made by EML are uploaded via OLIMS to a new IT0015 “WC External Earnings” wagetype on the detached record, offsetting EPSS top-up using WT4901 reduction rules. Controlled overrides (OLIMS/PA30) are role-restricted, audited, and validated (one-per-day/date, within active period, payroll-lock blocking).
- Salary & indexation: Period 1/2 salary bases derive from the last active IT0008 of the original employee (capacity utilisation retained); Award rises stop at termination. CPI or approved index is applied annually to Period 2/3 and the maximum monthly benefit (75% loaded salary basis), and eligible pre-termination retros from the original record update detached entitlements.
- Week counting: S36/S37 week counting and related reporting/eligibility checks continue without WT4901 by using WC certificates and the IT0015 external earnings wagetype as external indicators; logic covers all detached scenarios to keep officers in statutory reporting and EPSS eligibility.
- Audit/compliance: All uploads, infotype creations, suppressions, overrides, and indexation events are audit-trailed with role-based access controls; OLIMS, SAP, and EML records are reconciled; documented manual remediation covers late/out-of-sequence terminations, hires, certificates, number assignment, and wagetype corrections.

## 5. Functional Requirements

### 5.1 Master Data & Hire
Detached officers are rehired as non-employees via PA40 “EPSS On Duty Detached” (baseline PBRI ZM) or an equivalent programmatic action, creating mandatory infotypes IT0000/0001/0002/0006/0007/IT0008 (derived from the last active record of the original employee, capacity retained, no future Award rises), IT0009, IT0105 FREM (prior employee link, TBC), IT0014 9WCO, and IT0015 WC External Earnings plus standard comms/tax/working time. A dedicated personnel number (e.g., 5000xxxx, max 8 chars) is auto-assigned with manual fallback in the same range (e.g., “05” + old number) and uniqueness/audit checks; entries are created only in active periods, with any pre-termination retros processed on the original number. Officers sit in a non-employee position with ESG “EPSS On Duty Detached” (fallback ESG XV); PBRI detached (3000 series) stays manual/out of automation. ESG/number range automation excludes detached officers from SAP access, NSWPF email, and directories. Manual remediation steps cover late/out-of-sequence terminations or hires, including re-pointing IT0014/IT0015 and certificate linkage to the detached number. All hires, number assignments, and master-data updates are audit-trailed with user/date/time/action and restricted to authorised roles; claims-officer IT0008 override is not permitted (DOA011), and OLIMS overrides must retain supporting documentation.

### 5.2 Claim Status & OLIMS Case Management
OLIMS maintains a “Reopen” claim status on the original employee number (not a new incident) with selectable Period 1/2/3 substatus, timestamping and auditing each change. The prior employee number (IT0105 FREM) is shown so certificates and actions reference both original and detached numbers. Post-termination certificates can be uploaded only when a detached record exists, are tied to the existing claim on the original number, linked to the detached number for post-termination periods, and must not trigger WC payroll; pre-termination retros link to the original number. Single/bulk uploads validate detached existence, date range, dual linkage, and duplicates; errors halt processing, are surfaced to claims officers, and are audit-logged, while successes proceed independently and must never trigger payroll. IT0014 9WCO created from detached certificates resides on the detached number only (never on the original post-termination) and must never trigger WT4901; SAP payroll suppresses WT4901 for detached officers and only allows retros on the original number. Late/out-of-sequence scenarios have documented, audited manual redirection (e.g., when termination/hire timing is out of order). S36/S37 week counting updates on certificate upload/status changes using external WC indicators when WT4901 is absent, with manual correction if needed. Access to uploads/status changes is restricted to authorised users, and regular reconciliation across OLIMS, SAP, and EML ensures compliance. If Reopen status or a valid detached number is missing, processing is halted and flagged for review.

### 5.3 WC Certificate Handling (OLIMS → SAP)
OLIMS only allows WC certificate uploads when a detached non-employee record exists in SAP; uploads stay with the existing claim/incident on the original number and link both original and detached numbers (IT0105 FREM shown for traceability). Certificates are date-validated and routed by period: pre-termination certificates go to the original number, create IT0014 9WCO on that record, and may drive approved retros; post-termination certificates go to the detached number, create IT0014 9WCO there, and must never trigger WT4901. ESG/number-range checks and duplicate detection apply to single and bulk uploads; errors halt processing, are surfaced to claims officers, and are audit-logged, while valid records proceed independently and must not trigger payroll for detached periods. WT4901 is suppressed for detached officers; only pre-termination retros on the original number may generate WT4901. External WC earnings from EML are loaded to the new IT0015 “WC External Earnings” wagetype on the detached record; these serve as indicators for week counting and offsets when WT4901 is absent. Late/out-of-sequence cases have documented, audited manual redirection to correct linkage and week counting when termination/hire timing or uploads are out of order. Week counting for S36/S37 relies on external indicators (certificates/IT0015) when WT4901 is absent. All uploads, IT0014 creations, suppressions, linkage actions, overrides (with supporting documentation), and errors are audit-logged, and reporting covers certificate/linkage activity, week counting, offsets, and compliance reconciliation.

### 5.4 IT0014 9WCO Creation Rules
Pre-termination certificates create IT0014 9WCO on the original employee number and may generate WT4901 under standard rules. Post-termination certificates create IT0014 9WCO only on the detached non-employee number and must never generate WT4901; payroll suppresses and blocks WT4901 for all detached periods (ESG/number-range controlled). Upload (single/bulk) is blocked and logged if a detached record is missing, dates are invalid, or duplicates/overlaps are detected; errors surface to claims officers. Future-dated IT0014 9WCO must be reassigned to the detached number if termination occurs before its start, with payment blocked until correctly assigned and flagged if the detached record is missing. P905 regeneration checks active status period-by-period and (re)creates IT0014 9WCO on the correct number without overlap or duplicates. Any attempt to create or allow WT4901 on detached numbers is blocked and logged as an error; only pre-termination on the original number may produce WT4901. Late/out-of-sequence cases follow documented, audited manual steps to reassign/correct IT0014 when termination/hire timing or uploads are out of order. S36/S37 week counting for detached officers uses external indicators (certificates/IT0015) rather than WT4901. All IT0014 9WCO create/update/delete/suppress actions (including WT4901 suppression) and related errors/overrides are audit-logged with source (OLIMS/PA30/interface), user/time, linked certificates, and employee numbers.

### 5.5 Payroll Suppression & Week Counting
WT4901 must be suppressed in payroll for all detached officers, controlled by ESG/number range; no WT4901 is generated on detached records. Retro payroll is allowed only for pre-termination periods on the original employee number; any retro after termination is blocked and logged. IT0014 9WCO on detached numbers must never trigger WT4901, and IT0015 “WC External Earnings” entries are used both to offset EPSS and as external indicators for S36/S37 week counting when WT4901 is absent. Late or out-of-sequence data (termination/hire/certificates/uploads) requires documented, audited manual corrections to redirect IT0014/IT0015 and preserve suppression and week counting. Bulk uploads validate detached linkage and suppression rules; errors (missing detached record, invalid dates, duplicate/overlap, payroll trigger) halt processing, surface to claims officers, and are audit-logged. All WT4901 suppressions, week-count inclusions (from certificates/IT0015), IT0014/IT0015 creations, errors, and manual interventions are audit-logged with user/date/time/action, and reporting shows suppressed WT4901 and week-count outcomes for the detached cohort.

### 5.6 WC External Earnings (IT0015)
A new wagetype “WC External Earnings” (code TBC) is created in IT0015 and allowed only for detached officers (ESG/number-range controlled); it is not permitted in IT0008 or IT0014. Source is EML upload via OLIMS containing detached number, original number, dates, amounts, and supporting documents. OLIMS validates active period, ESG/number range, and one entry per day/date; duplicates are rejected with clear errors, and QA reports check for duplicates. Entries must fall within the detached active period. The wagetype offsets EPSS using WT4901 reduction logic and serves as the external WC indicator for S36/S37 when WT4901 is absent; if WT4901 exists in the same period, offsets are combined. Entries from OLIMS and PA30 are visible in both SAP (PA30) and OLIMS IT15 with a source indicator; supporting docs are retained for OLIMS and SAP standard audit applies for PA30. Overrides (create/update/delete) by authorised users via OLIMS or PA30 are flagged separately with user/date/time/reason; no create/update/delete is allowed during payroll lock (blocked and logged). All entries, overrides, errors, and duplicates are audit-logged, and reporting lists WC External Earnings with source, date, amount, EPSS impact, overrides, duplicates, and errors.

### 5.7 Manual Override of WC External Earnings
Authorised EPSS Claims Officers may create/update/delete WC External Earnings via OLIMS or PA30 for insurer adjustments or retrospective corrections (not for Award/IT0008 changes). Overrides are flagged separately from uploads with user/date/time/reason and a source indicator (OLIMS or PA30). Standard validations apply: detached ESG/number range only, within the active period, one entry per day, duplicates rejected; overrides are blocked during payroll control record lock and attempts are logged. All overrides are visible in OLIMS and PA30; supporting documentation is retained for OLIMS entries, and SAP audit applies for PA30. Errors (invalid dates, duplicates, unauthorised access) are logged, and only authorised users may perform overrides, ensuring compliance and traceability.

### 5.8 Salary Derivation & Indexation (IT0008 logic)
Period 1/2/3 salary bases for detached officers are derived from the last active IT0008 of the original employee, retaining capacity utilisation; the detached IT0008 is frozen and receives no Award updates. Period 1 ORP uses the last active salary at termination; Period 2 base is the Period 1 salary indexed annually from the original P2 start; Period 3 follows scheme rules using the indexed values. The seven-year clock continues from the original P2 start regardless of any gap; RTD/PID extensions entered in OLIMS flow to SAP to adjust the clock. CPI/indexation is applied annually to the 75% loaded salary and the maximum monthly benefit; if the current rate is unavailable, the prior-year rate is used until updated. All Award increases post-termination are excluded. Eligible retros for pre-termination periods are processed on the original number and update detached P1/P2/P3 outcomes; retros for post-termination periods are blocked. All derivations, indexation events/rates, retro adjustments, seven-year clock status/extensions, and effective dates are audit-logged and reportable; indexation overrides are restricted and fully audited.

### 5.9 Indexation of Period 2 & 3 (Detached)
Apply the approved annual indexation rate (e.g., CPI) to both the 75% loaded salary and the maximum monthly benefit, effective from the scheme’s indexation date. If the current rate is unavailable, use the prior-year rate until updated. Indexation is strictly separate from Award rises; all Award increases post-termination are blocked. Indexed values feed all Period 2/3 entitlement calculations and reporting. All indexation actions (rate, effective date, user, impacted records) are audit-logged; any manual override is restricted to authorised payroll administrators and must be fully auditable.

### 5.10 Late / Out-of-Sequence Data
Scenarios include late termination/hire after certificates or IT0014 exist, late certificates, and future-dated IT0014 when termination occurs earlier. System validations check detached record existence and date alignment; uploads/IT0014 creation for post-termination periods are blocked and logged if the detached record is missing. Bulk uploads validate each record independently; errors do not block successful records. Manual steps are documented to redirect/correct IT0014 9WCO and certificate linkage, reassign/delete future-dated IT0014 if termination precedes start, and hold/process items once the detached record exists. Retro logic: pre-termination late certificates trigger retro payroll on the original number only; post-termination certificates must not trigger payroll. Week counting (S36/S37) must be corrected when records are adjusted. All corrections, reassignments, deletions, and blocked actions are audit-logged with user/time/action/reason and surfaced to claims officers for resolution.

### 5.11 Bulk Uploads
Bulk upload in OLIMS supports WC certificates and external earnings for detached officers with per-record validation: confirm detached record exists (block post-termination uploads if missing), enforce correct linkage to original and detached numbers, validate date ranges, enforce one certificate/external earning per day, and prevent duplicates. WT4901 is suppressed and payroll triggers are blocked for detached officers on all uploaded records. Each record processes independently; errors (missing detached record, invalid dates, duplicates, payroll trigger attempts) are reported per record to claims officers and audit-logged, while successful records proceed and link to the existing claim/incident (no new incidents created). Week counting and claim linkage are maintained for valid uploads. All successes and errors are audit-logged with user/date/time/action; failed records can be corrected and re-uploaded.

### 5.12 Error Handling & Audit Trail
Log errors (missing detached record, invalid certificate/earnings dates, duplicate uploads, suppression breaches, attempted WT4901 on detached, payroll-lock violations) with user/time/detail and notify relevant claims/payroll users. Maintain a comprehensive audit trail for uploads, IT0014/IT0015 create/update/delete/suppress, overrides, indexation events, and manual corrections, capturing source (OLIMS/PA30/interface), user, date/time, and action details. Enforce access controls so only authorised users may upload/override/correct; all access attempts are logged. Bulk processes audit both successes and failures; supporting documentation for OLIMS overrides is retained and linked. Reporting draws from the audit trail for compliance, reconciliation, and regulatory needs.

### 5.13 Reporting & Compliance
Provide reports that filter the detached cohort by ESG/number range (e.g., 5000xxxx/3100) and cover: certificate lifecycle (upload/validation/linkage/status/audit), IT0014/IT0015 entries with period, user, source (OLIMS/PA30/interface), WT4901 suppressions/attempts, external earnings offsets and overrides (one-per-day, supporting docs, impact), indexation events (rate, effective date, user, overrides), and week-count outcomes using external indicators when WT4901 is absent. Generate exception reports for out-of-sequence/late data, unlinked/misallocated records, number range/ESG/suppression breaches, duplicate/invalid external earnings, and manual interventions with reasons. Reconciliation between OLIMS, SAP, and EML confirms no post-termination WC payroll and correct offsets. Compliance controls verified in reports: no Award increases post-termination, only eligible retros processed, indexation overrides restricted/audited, validated number range/ESG usage, and full audit trail leveraged. Outputs must be exportable for regulatory/statutory reporting and audits.

## 6. Exception Handling
- Retro WC payments are permitted only for pre-termination periods on the original employee number; any post-termination WC via SAP is invalid and must be blocked/suppressed and logged.
- Post-termination WC payments processed in SAP are invalid; WT4901 is suppressed for detached officers, and any attempt to generate/process it is blocked and logged as an error.
- Numbering/ESG/linkage failures (missing/invalid personnel number, ESG, or IT0105 FREM link) halt hire/upload/payment and are flagged for manual resolution.
- If the approved indexation rate is unavailable, the prior-year rate is applied temporarily, and the exception is logged for follow-up.
- Week counting must not rely solely on WT4901; if no external WC indicator is present, flag for manual review to ensure statutory compliance.
- Late/out-of-sequence data (termination/hire/certificate/IT0014/IT0015 order issues) are blocked or held; manual intervention redirects/corrects records to the appropriate number/period and is logged.
- Payroll lock blocks uploads/overrides/changes to certificates/IT0014/IT0015; attempts are logged.
- Future-dated IT0014 after termination are suppressed, reassigned to the detached number if applicable, and logged for review to prevent invalid payments.

## 7. Compliance/Controls
Payroll must enforce WT4901 suppression for all detached officers via ESG/number range, allowing retro payments only for pre-termination periods on the original number. Access to uploads, overrides, and configuration (number range/ESG) is restricted to authorised roles; detached officers remain excluded from SAP/email/downstream systems via ESG/number range. Number range/ESG configuration is documented, transported, and auditable, preventing duplicate or out-of-range assignments. All actions—certificate uploads, IT0014/IT0015 creation, wagetype suppressions, claim status changes, overrides, config changes, and errors—are audit-logged with user/time/action. QA reports detect duplicate WC External Earnings (one per day), wagetype misassignment, and number range/ESG compliance issues; exceptions are flagged for resolution. Regular OLIMS/SAP/EML reconciliations confirm no post-termination WC payroll and correct offsets. Indexation is applied automatically; overrides are restricted/audited; Award increases post-termination are blocked. Separation of duties covers configuration, data entry, overrides, and approvals. All SAP configuration is governed by transport and documentation processes, with current process guides and reviewed/tested/signed-off changes. Controls and reporting collectively support compliance and regulatory auditability.

## 8. Acceptance Criteria (Aggregated)
Detached officers are hired as non-employees with a new unique number (e.g., 5000xxxx/3100) and assigned to a non-employee position/ESG “EPSS On Duty Detached”; the prior employee number is linked in IT0105 FREM; only authorised users may perform hires, and detached officers are excluded from SAP/email/downstream. OLIMS supports “Reopen” with Period 1/2/3 on the original number, validates a detached record before post-termination uploads, and links certificates/claims to both original and detached numbers. WC certificates are stored; IT0014 9WCO is created on the detached number for post-termination periods; pre-termination retros are on the original number only; no post-termination IT0014 on the original; retro WC only pre-termination. WT4901 is suppressed for detached officers; no SAP WC payments post-termination (EML pays); no Award increases post-termination. The new IT0015 “WC External Earnings” wagetype offsets EPSS (WT4901 logic), enforces one-per-day/date and active-period validation, and supports audited overrides with payroll-lock protection. Salary/indexation: P1/P2/P3 derive from the last active IT0008 (capacity utilisation retained); annual CPI/indexation on P2/3 and max benefit; eligible pre-termination retros update calculations; Award rises blocked. Week counting for S36/S37 works without WT4901 by using external WC indicators. Bulk uploads are validated with per-record errors; all actions (uploads, IT0014/IT0015, suppressions, overrides) are audit logged; reconciliations OLIMS/SAP/EML and compliance reports are available. Exception controls cover retro only pre-termination, documented late/out-of-sequence handling, WT4901 post-termination blocked, indexation fallback, and all exceptions logged/auditable. UAT and sign-off by business and technical stakeholders confirm all requirements.
